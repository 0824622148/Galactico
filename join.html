Player assessments  ·  U6 – U13 (born 2013 – 2020)

Take the next step and become part of a football environment focused on development, discipline and opportunity.

How it works

01
Register with GSSI Ormonde
Registration is handled through our development partner, GSSI Ormonde.
02
Attend training sessions
Players train year-round, including through the off season.
03
Be assessed for squad selection
GSSI and Galactico United coaches assess players for 2026 squad selection.

Why join Galactico United?

Professional coaching and philosophy-driven development.
All year-round training, not a single seasonal window.
A commitment to excellence and holistic long-term player development.
Competitive SLFA Premier League football from U6 to U15.
What parents should expect
Assessments take place at GSSI Ormonde with GSSI and Galactico United coaches. Training schedules, fees and kit information are confirmed directly with the club on enquiry.
